Beyond Harry Potter

J.K. Rowling has written far more than wizard stories. She has published an adult novel, a children's fairy tale, and a companion book that began as a textbook inside the Harry Potter world.

The Casual Vacancy

2012

Her first novel for adults is set in the small English town of Pagford. When a beloved councillor suddenly dies, a fight over his empty seat exposes the secrets, rivalries, and prejudices of the whole community.

The Ickabog

2020

This fairy tale for children takes place in the kingdom of Cornucopia, where people tell stories about a terrifying monster in the northern marshes. Rowling first shared it online for free in chapters during the 2020 lockdowns, then published it as a book that November.

Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them

2001

Presented as a Hogwarts textbook written by the wizard Newt Scamander, this guide describes magical creatures from the Niffler to the Hippogriff. It was published to raise money for the charity Comic Relief, and it later inspired a film series.
