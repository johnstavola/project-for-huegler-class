The Harry Potter Series

Seven books follow Harry from a cupboard under the stairs to the final battle against Voldemort. Here are four of the most memorable.

Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone

1997

On his eleventh birthday, Harry learns he is a wizard and is invited to Hogwarts. He makes his first friends, Ron and Hermione, and uncovers a secret about the stone hidden inside the school. (Published in the US as the Sorcerer's Stone.)

Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban

1999

Sirius Black has escaped from the wizard prison of Azkaban, and everyone believes he is coming for Harry. This darker third book introduces Professor Lupin, the Dementors, and a very unusual way to be in two places at once.

Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire

2000

Hogwarts hosts the dangerous Triwizard Tournament, and Harry's name mysteriously comes out of the Goblet even though he is too young to compete. The book ends with the return of Lord Voldemort, changing the series for good.

Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows

2007

In the final book, Harry, Ron, and Hermione leave school to hunt down the Horcruxes that keep Voldemort alive. It all builds to the battle of Hogwarts and the end of the story.
