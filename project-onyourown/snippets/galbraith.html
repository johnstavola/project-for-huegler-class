The Cormoran Strike Novels

Writing as Robert Galbraith, J.K. Rowling created a gritty detective series about private investigator Cormoran Strike and his partner Robin Ellacott. The author's true identity was revealed shortly after the first book came out. Here are three of the books.

The Cuckoo's Calling

2013

A famous model falls to her death from her London balcony, and the police rule it a suicide. Her brother doesn't believe it and hires Strike, a war veteran with a struggling agency, to look deeper. Robin arrives as a temp and quickly becomes essential.

Career of Evil

2015

Robin opens a package and finds a woman's severed leg inside. Strike realizes the sender could be one of three men from his past, and the pair race to find the killer before he strikes again. This is the darkest book of the series so far.

Troubled Blood

2020

Strike takes on a cold case: a young doctor who vanished without a trace in 1974. With decades-old evidence and a long list of suspects, he and Robin dig into one of the hardest mysteries they have faced. It is also the longest book in the series.
