J.K. Rowling

Author of the Harry Potter series

About

Joanne "J.K." Rowling was born on July 31, 1965, in Yate, England. She wrote the Harry Potter series, which became one of the best-selling book series in history and inspired a hugely successful film franchise.

She has also written adult fiction, including The Casual Vacancy, and crime novels under the pen name Robert Galbraith.
